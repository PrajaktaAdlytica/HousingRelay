HousingRelay pilot handoff

Required deliverables / research-stage draft / no live safety controls

01 · Brief

HousingRelay / Concept 44 / logistics and knowledge operations. Buyer: portfolio asset or maintenance director with building-level inspection obligations and pilot budget authority. Initial wedge: Repair Proof deficiency-to-work-order reconciliation over the existing CMMS, not rent/accounting replacement. Five separate proposed paid modules: Repair Desk intake/deduplication; Repair Review human hazard routing; Repair Proof reconciliation; Repair Signal contractor/parts/access coordination; Repair Report evidence audit and recurrence. Proposed six-week one-building fee $750; later $150/building/month, both unvalidated hypotheses.

02 · Deficiency fixture

HM-032 / fictional B-03 / masked unit •B (canonical synthetic 2B) links to WO-811. Work order complete 19 Oct 2026 at 11:20 -04:00; invoice 11:25; technician photo missing; supervisor verification pending; resident notification held. Wrong-unit WO-820 is a rejected candidate. The CSV and JSON include 10 records and two historical illustrative documentation reviews. Photo references in those records are fictional placeholders, not actual repair images.

03 · Supervisor storyboard

Queue → select HM-032 → view inspection and work-order source → inspect missing evidence → request evidence in local simulation → retain verification pending → reopen review if needed → inspect illustrative report. Guided story separately rehearses match, illustrative evidence reference, acknowledgement and explicit human documentation review. No autonomous hazard closeout.

04 · Resident storyboard

Resident opens only their synthetic ticket → reads follow-up pending → switches English/Spanish → acknowledges update locally → sees clear emergency boundary. Never show other residents, invoices, internal notes or the building queue. Acknowledgement is not consent or safety clearance.

05 · Annotated capture specification

Capture the real rendered prototype at 1440px desktop and 390px mobile, at 100% zoom. Required: list/queue; one opened HM-032; evidence/source pane; reviewer controls; report; own-ticket resident view. Keep DEMO — SYNTHETIC DATA and CONCEPT PREVIEW on every frame, mask unit, show timestamps and verification pending. Label charts illustrative. No HUD seal, resident photograph or implied certification. Image descriptions must explain the depicted workflow. Existing source screenshot is only a concept reference.

06 · Interview guide

Interview 10 operators/supervisors. Ask: Walk through the last inspection deficiency. How was its work order found? Who checked the closeout evidence? What caused the last revisit? Is documentation or scheduling the actual bottleneck? Which API/export and attachment entitlements exist? How much duplicate entry occurs? Who owns the budget? Can you name a supervisor and participating contractor? What additional field minutes are acceptable? Ask contractors about staffing, parts and access; compliance staff about program/notice mapping and local tenant requirements.

08 · Shadow-pilot scorecard and commitment

Weeks 1–2: operator and contractor permission, DPA, named supervisor, baseline, approved on-call process. Weeks 3–5: shadow actual incoming tickets only in separately authorized environment; compare current CMMS with proposed workflow using agreed denominators and windows. Week 6: review rework, audit effort, field burden and evidence gaps. Seek paid commitment. Build integrations only if paid pilot shows incremental value and on-call process accepts the workflow. Stop on unsafe routing, inaccessible resident view or unmanageable field burden.

09 · Acquisition, evidence and expansion

Acquisition hypotheses: affordable-housing associations, compliance consultants, housing-authority procurement, contractor networks and property-manager referrals; private portfolios may pilot sooner. Publish closeout checklist with official HUD links and program caveat. LinkedIn tests compare closed tickets without evidence with orphaned inspection items; capped pre-approved spend, no resident targeting or distress creative. Opportunity stages: Operator interview → Data terms → Shadow building → Paid pilot. Strong evidence for regulatory workflows and entrenched products; low evidence for incremental AI value. Expand by retained properties/units, preventive maintenance and inspection preparation only on demonstrated lower rework/audit cost; no unsupported per-unit price or national TAM.

10 · Identity, scope and implementation status

HousingRelay supersedes historical Repair Waypoint / repairwaypoint.com. Historical RDAP 404 was not a purchase or trademark clearance. No demonstrated demand, customers, revenue, live integrations, security certification, pricing acceptance or operating service. Original illustrations and synthetic records only. No production tenant app, emergency dispatch, payment, HUD submission or automatic hazard resolution. Proposed analytics: deficiency-demo open, safety disclosure viewed, pilot form submit with source/campaign attribution; no resident PII. No connected analytics or CRM. Qualified buyer commitment, not traffic, determines validation.
