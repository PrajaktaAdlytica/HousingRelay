## Boundaries accounted for, not unfinished production promises

The brief explicitly excludes a production product and deployment. Real authentication, resident isolation enforcement, email delivery, live integrations, payment, analytics, legal approval, domain clearance, campaigns and real pilot results remain unconnected/unvalidated and are described honestly. The requested sign-in is a complete frontend preview. Existing historical photo references are labeled illustrative placeholders, never verified evidence. No testimonials or customers were invented to fill optional master-guide examples.
